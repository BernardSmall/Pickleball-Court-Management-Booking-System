# Acceptance Checklist

- Exactly six courts.
- No Perks page or perk logic.
- Calendar-first booking.
- Monday-Saturday 09:00-19:00.
- Sunday 15:00-19:00.
- Club Credits use R1 = 1 Credit.
- Member gets 1 free hour per membership month.
- Pickle Pro gets 2 hours in each of 3 separate membership months.
- Free booking leaves wallet unchanged.
- Qualifying cancellation restores/refunds once.
- Expired paid memberships fall back to Play & Go benefits.
- Admin can manage bookings, courts, blocks, hours, memberships and wallet adjustments.
- Help & Tour uses stable modal/page descriptions.
- Desktop, mobile, accessibility and Back/Forward behaviour are tested.
